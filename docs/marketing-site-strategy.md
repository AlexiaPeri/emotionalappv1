# Emerge marketing site strategy

The public product site lives in `site/`. It is separate from the app prototype (`index.html`) and the private writing studio (`writing.html`).

## Audiences

1. Potential users who need to understand the experience quickly.
2. Entrepreneurship programmes evaluating the clarity and relevance of the concept.
3. Potential partners and early investors looking for a differentiated mechanism, credible positioning and an honest product stage.

## Narrative

1. **Offer:** a voice-led practice for emotional release.
2. **Need:** distraction is readily available when emotions rise; direct, contained practices are not.
3. **Mechanism:** feel, say, hear, repeat.
4. **Product:** a quiet interface designed to make the screen disappear during practice.
5. **Differentiation:** no advice, interpretation or generative rewriting of the user's words.
6. **Evidence:** related mechanisms are supported by research; the Emerge repetition loop remains a product hypothesis to validate.
7. **Vision:** make emotional release as available as distraction.

## Evidence boundary

The site must never imply that studies of affect labeling, suppression or movement prove that Emerge itself is effective. Product outcomes require dedicated beta research. Emerge is positioned as a wellness practice, not a medical device, diagnosis or replacement for professional care.
