# Future Guide step layout

This document preserves a visual direction that was removed from the public marketing site but may improve the in-app Guide later.

## Purpose

The exact practice should be taught only when a person is preparing to try it. The public site establishes why the experience matters; the Guide can then explain how to do it well.

## Layout to preserve

- A warm, dark full-width section with generous breathing room.
- One clear introductory title and a short orienting sentence.
- Four distinct numbered steps rather than one uninterrupted block of instructions.
- On desktop, four equal columns. On iPhone, a calm vertical sequence with strong separation between steps.
- Each step uses a small number, a short action word, and no more than two concise sentences.
- Fine copper rules may separate the steps. Avoid nested cards and heavy borders.
- If a voice example is useful, place it after the conceptual introduction and immediately before the steps. Keep the user's line and Emerge's response visually distinct.
- The interface should feel instructional and reassuring, not technical.

## Earlier content structure

The earlier concept used the sequence `Feel / Say / Hear / Repeat`. Those labels are a reference, not final copy. The Guide rewrite should prioritize the actual learning needs of a first-time user and may use more precise language.

## Public-site boundary

Do not reuse the detailed call-and-response demonstration on the public marketing site. It reveals the technical mechanic before the visitor understands the role of focus, movement and sustained emotional contact.
