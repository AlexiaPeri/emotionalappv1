# English V1 recording scripts

These four recordings are the complete prerecorded guidance set for the first English beta. The separate first-session guide has been removed; the first-session recording now welcomes the user, explains the method briefly and leads directly into the first phrase.

## Recording setup

- Record in the quietest, least reflective room available.
- Keep the microphone position and input level unchanged across all four files.
- Record dry voice only: no music, reverb, compression or noise reduction.
- Preferred delivery format: mono WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit. A high-quality original M4A is also acceptable if WAV is not available.
- Leave two seconds of room tone before and after every take.
- Record two complete takes of each script. Do not splice sentences yourself.
- Text in square brackets is a direction or silence and must not be spoken.

## 01 · First session

Target filename: `01-first-session.wav`

Target duration: 70–90 seconds, including silence.

Delivery: warm and direct. This is an invitation, not a performance or a lesson.

> Welcome to your first session of Emerge.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> The most important thing is to let yourself be as you are. Do not try to change or push what you feel. If you become bored, self-conscious, or uncomfortable, notice that too. There is no need to judge yourself or perform. Keep bringing your attention back to what you feel.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> Let your body take part. As you express yourself with your voice, allow yourself to move in whatever way feels natural.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> The practice is simple. Say a short sentence about how you feel. The app changes “I” to “you” where needed and repeats the sentence back to you. Then say it again. Stay with the sentence until another one arises naturally as your feelings change.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> If it becomes too intense, tap “I need support.” You can stop at any time.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> Now, let’s begin.
>
> [Pause 2 seconds]
>
> Close your eyes if that feels comfortable.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> Take a slow breath in, then breathe out through your mouth.
>
> [Pause 5 seconds]
>
> Bring your attention inward.
>
> [Pause 4 seconds]
>
> What do you feel?

## 03 · Closing

Target filename: `03-closing.wav`

Target duration: 70–90 seconds, including silence.

Delivery: slower than the openings. Do not imply that the user should feel better or that the session had a particular result.

> The practice is ending now.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> Keep your eyes closed if that feels comfortable.
>
> [Pause 4 seconds]
>
> Let your body be supported by the position you are in.
>
> [Pause 6 seconds]
>
> Notice your breathing without changing it.
>
> [Pause 6 seconds]
>
> Feel your feet. Feel your hands. Notice where your body meets the floor, the chair, or the bed.
>
> [Pause 8 seconds]
>
> Let your body integrate what it has just experienced. There is nothing to understand or solve right now.
>
> [Pause 10 seconds]
>
> Begin to notice the sounds in the room. Invite a small movement into your fingers and toes.
>
> [Pause 8 seconds]
>
> When you are ready, open your eyes.
>
> [Pause 6 seconds]
>
> Take all the time you need. You can reflect on what emerged, or simply end the session.

## 04 · Grounding

Target filename: `04-grounding.wav`

Target duration: 75–100 seconds, including silence.

Delivery: concrete, steady and unhurried. Avoid sounding alarmed. The user may stop this recording at any time.

> Pause. The practice has stopped.
>
> [Pause 3 seconds]
>
> Find the most comfortable position you can.
>
> [Pause 5 seconds]
>
> Feel the support beneath your body.
>
> [Pause 6 seconds]
>
> If it feels comfortable, place a hand on your chest or anywhere that feels steady.
>
> [Pause 6 seconds]
>
> Notice your breathing without needing to change it.
>
> [Pause 6 seconds]
>
> Let your exhale become a little longer.
>
> [Pause 8 seconds]
>
> Look around and notice three things you can see.
>
> [Pause 8 seconds]
>
> Feel your feet or your hands. Invite a small movement into your fingers or toes.
>
> [Pause 8 seconds]
>
> You are here, and you can take all the time you need.
>
> [Pause 8 seconds]
>
> When you are ready, choose whether you feel a little better or still need support.

## Handoff

Send both complete takes of all four recordings. Do not trim silence or convert the files after recording. The selected takes will be cleaned, level-matched, trimmed and integrated into the native app together.
